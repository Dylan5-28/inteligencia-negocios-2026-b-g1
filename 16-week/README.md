## Dashboard & insight

This interactive Power BI dashboard visualizes core sales performance metrics across regions and product lines during 2025. 
The solution incorporates custom slicers and a multi-level date hierarchy enabling seamless drill-down from annual trends to quarterly breakdown. 
Dynamic visual cross-filtering reveals that the North region drives the primary sales volume, reaching its peak in the third quarter (Q3). 
Conversely, the South region presents untapped growth opportunities requiring target allocation. 
It is strongly recommended to optimize inventory management and realign marketing investments to capitalize on high-performing quarters.
